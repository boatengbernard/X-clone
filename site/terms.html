Terms of Use

Last updated 9 November 2026 · Draft

This is a plain-language draft for Selah’s preview. Have it reviewed by a lawyer in your country before the public launch, and add a contact address for questions and reports.

These terms are an agreement between you and Selah. By creating an account or using Selah, you agree to them. If you do not agree, please do not use Selah.

1. Who can join

Anyone aged 13 or older can create an account. If the law where you live sets a higher age for using online services without a parent’s consent, you need to meet that age or have that consent. One person, one account, and the details you give us must be true.

2. What Selah is for

Selah is a place to share Scripture, devotions, sermons, prayer requests and honest questions with believers from every tradition, and with seekers and guests who want to listen. Disagreeing about belief is welcome. Treating people with contempt is not.

3. How to treat others

When you post or reply, you agree not to:

  • mock, insult or demean anyone for their faith, tradition, doubt or lack of faith;
  • harass, threaten or bully anyone, or encourage others to;
  • share hateful content about people because of who they are;
  • pretend to be someone else, or mislead people about who you are;
  • post spam, scams, advertising or links that harm devices;
  • share anything sexual involving minors, or any illegal content;
  • share other people’s private information without their permission.

4. Your posts

You own what you post. By posting, you give Selah permission to store it, show it to other members and display it in feeds, for as long as it stays on Selah. You can delete your posts at any time. Only post things you have the right to share, and credit Scripture translations and sermons where it matters.

5. Reports and moderation

Any member can report a post. Moderators review reports and may remove posts that break these terms. If someone breaks them repeatedly or seriously, we may suspend or close their account. Posts are never removed only because they express a different belief.

6. Shared videos and links

Selah lets you share links from YouTube, Instagram, X, TikTok and Snapchat. Those videos belong to their creators and are governed by those services’ own terms. A video only loads from the other service after you tap play.

7. No ads

Selah does not show ads and does not sell your attention or your data. If we ever introduce paid features, we will explain them openly before they affect you.

8. Closing your account

You can stop using Selah and ask us to delete your account at any time. We may close an account that breaks these terms, and we will tell you why when we can.

9. Responsibility

Selah is provided as it is. We work to keep it running and safe, but we cannot promise it will always be available or free of mistakes. Posts reflect the views of the people who write them, not of Selah. Nothing on Selah is professional medical, legal or counselling advice.

10. Changes

If we change these terms in a meaningful way, we will tell members in the app before the change takes effect.
