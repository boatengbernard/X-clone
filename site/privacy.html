Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 November 2026 · Draft

This is a plain-language draft for Selah’s preview. Have it reviewed by a lawyer before the public launch, especially for the countries your members live in, and add a contact address for privacy requests.

Selah has no ads and nothing to sell, so we collect only what we need to run the community. This page explains what that is and what you can do about it.

What we collect

InformationWhy we need it
Name, username and emailTo create your account, show who wrote a post, and let you log in.
PasswordTo protect your account. We store a scrambled (hashed) version, never the password itself.
How you identify (for example Catholic, Baptist, Seeker)Shown on your profile and posts, and used for the tradition filter and the “view from another tradition” label. You can change it at any time.
Your posts, replies, amens, prayers, bookmarks and followsTo show your activity to you and to other members, and to build your feeds.
Reports you sendSo moderators can review posts that may break the rules.
Photos you uploadResized on your device and stripped of location data before upload.

We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not build a profile of you to sell.

Your faith is sensitive information

Religious belief is a special category of personal data in many countries. Selah only uses the tradition you choose to run the features described above. We never sell it, never share it with advertisers, and never use it to target you.

Shared videos

When someone shares a YouTube, Instagram, X, TikTok or Snapchat link, nothing loads from that service until you tap play. Once you do, that service may collect information under its own privacy policy. “Open on …” takes you to the service directly.

Who can see what

  • Members can see your name, username, tradition, profile, posts and replies.
  • Your email and password are never shown to other members.
  • Moderators can see reports and the posts they are about.
  • Service providers that host Selah’s servers handle data only to run Selah for us.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete a post, it is removed from Selah. When you delete your account, we delete your profile and posts, except where the law requires us to keep something for longer.

Your choices

  • Edit your name, bio, location, website and tradition from your profile.
  • Delete any of your posts at any time.
  • Ask for a copy of your information, or for your account to be deleted.

The current preview

In today’s preview, accounts and posts are saved only in your own browser, on your device. Nothing is sent to a Selah server yet. Clearing your browser’s site data removes them.

Children

Selah is for people aged 13 and older. If you believe a younger child has made an account, tell us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will tell members in the app before it takes effect.

See also the Terms of Use.
