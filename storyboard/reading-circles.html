What we build first

  • Circles of 6–8, matched by plan, start date and timezone
  • Four starter plans, each 1–7 minutes a day
  • Daily reminder linked straight into the passage
  • Mark as read, plus an optional one-line share
  • Two grace days a week; progress never resets

Care and safety

  • Circle posts are private and never appear in the public feed
  • Volunteer guides who have completed a plan
  • Report and leave a circle at any time
  • Posts mentioning self-harm show crisis resources and alert the guide

How we'll know it works

  • Share of members still reading on day 7 and day 21
  • Return rate within 3 days after a missed day
  • Members who start a second plan
