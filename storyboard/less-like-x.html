1

Keep & Pass it on

Effort: small

Every action gets a word next to its icon. Bookmark becomes Keep, drawn as a Bible with a ribbon. Share becomes Pass it on, drawn as an open hand.

What changes
New Keep and Pass it on icons and labels. The Bookmarks page is renamed Kept. Repost moves into Pass it on, which also offers Copy link.
Why not X
X uses bare icons. Words make the row read like Selah, and the ribbon and hand shapes are Selah's own.
Trade-off
The row is busier. On small phones the words wrap onto two lines.

2

The Journal

Effort: medium

Bookmarking becomes Add to journal. When you save a post, you can write a private note about why it spoke to you. The Bookmarks tab becomes your Journal.

What changes
A notebook icon replaces the bookmark. Saving opens a sheet with an optional private note. The Journal page shows each saved post with your note and the date you saved it. Share moves into the ⋯ menu.
Why not X
X bookmarks are a silent pile of saved posts. A journal with your own reflections is a devotional habit and has no X equivalent.
Trade-off
It takes more building: notes need saving, plus a new Journal page layout.

3

One quiet menu

Effort: small

The row keeps only the three responses that matter on Selah: Amen, Praying and Reply. Keep, send, repost, copy link and report all move into a single ⋯ menu that slides up from the bottom.

What changes
Bookmark, share and repost leave the row, and the report flag leaves the top of each post. Tapping ⋯ opens one sheet with all of them.
Why not X
X's five-icon row disappears. What's left is calmer and puts Amen first, which fits "a calm social network".
Trade-off
Saving and sharing take one extra tap.

4

Verse cards

Effort: medium–large

Sharing a devotion turns it into a Scripture card: the passage in serif type, the member's reflection, and the Selah mark, ready to send in Selah or save as an image for WhatsApp or Instagram.

What changes
The share arrow becomes Pass it on. On devotions it opens the card preview. Other posts get the plain send and copy-link options.
Why not X
X shares a link. A Scripture card is something churches and friends already pass around, and it carries Selah's name with it, which also helps growth.
Trade-off
Drawing the image takes the most work of the five. Verse text also needs a Bible translation Selah is allowed to show.

5

Cards, not a timeline

Effort: medium

The biggest visual break from X. Posts become separate paper cards with space between them. Responses become soft pill buttons. Keeping a post drops a gold ribbon on the card's corner, like a ribbon marker in a Bible.

What changes
The feed, profile and search lists all switch from ruled rows to cards. The bookmark icon is gone: you tap and hold a card, or use ⋯, to keep it, and the ribbon shows it's kept. Pass it on becomes a quiet text link.
Why not X
X's look is a flat, ruled timeline. Cards with ribbons look like a devotional, not a newsfeed, even at a glance.
Trade-off
Fewer posts fit on screen, and it touches most pages, so it needs a careful pass on phones.
